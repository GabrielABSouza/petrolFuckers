## 5. AVAILABLE DATA SOURCES - How to Access

### SIDRA API (IBGE) - Best for programmatic access
Base URL: `https://apisidra.ibge.gov.br/values/`

**Key Tables:**
- **Tabela 1612** - PAM (Produção Agrícola Municipal): area, production, yield by municipality for all temporary crops
  - Example: `t/1612/p/2023/v/214/c81/2713/n3/all` (soy production by state)
  - v=214 (quantity produced), v=215 (area planted), v=216 (area harvested), v=217 (yield), v=218 (production value)
  - For municipality level: use `n6/all` instead of `n3/all`
  
- **Tabela 289** - PEVS (Produção da Extração Vegetal e Silvicultura): production quantity and value for vegetal extraction by municipality

- **Tabela 5938** - PIB by State (municipal level also available)
  - v=37 (PIB total), v=40 (VA services), v=45 (VA industry)

- **Tabela 6784** - PIB Municipal (GDP of Municipalities)
  - Full municipal GDP with industry/services/agriculture breakdown

- **Tabela 1846** - CEMPRE (Cadastro Central de Empresas): employment, wages by sector and municipality
  - Has data on number of companies, employees, wages by CNAE sector

- **PIA-Empresa** (Pesquisa Industrial Anual): industrial production, employment by sector/state
- **PIA-Produto**: industrial products by type/state

### SIDRA Interactive
URL: https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/
- Navigate to Economia > Indústria > PIA-Empresa or PIA-Produto
- Navigate to Economia > Agropecuária > PAM or PEVS
- Navigate to Economia > Contas Nacionais > PIB-Munic
- Navigate to Cadastro Central de Empresas - CEMPRE

### Observatório SEBRAE
URL: https://observatorio.sebrae.com.br/
- Has DataExplorer with employment by occupation, salary by economic division, companies by size
- Municipal profiles available ("Município em números")
- Sector profiles available

### Instituto Aço Brasil
URL: https://www.acobrasil.org.br/site/industry-data/
- Steel Databook 2025 available for download
- Monthly sector statistics
- Plant locations by state/municipality

### Recommended Next Steps for Municipal-Level Data
1. **PIB Municipal**: `https://apisidra.ibge.gov.br/values/t/6784/p/2022/v/9812/n6/all` (all municipalities GDP)
2. **PAM by Municipality**: `https://apisidra.ibge.gov.br/values/t/1612/p/2023/v/214/c81/2713/n6/-all` (soy by municipality)
3. **CEMPRE Employment**: Use Tabela 1846 with CNAE codes for manufacturing sectors
4. **PEVS by Municipality**: Use Tabela 289 for forestry/vegetal extraction data (biomass potential)
5. **PIA by State/Sector**: Industrial annual survey with production value, employment, wages by manufacturing subsector

### CNAE 2.0 Codes for Key Industrial Sectors
- C20: Fabricação de produtos químicos
- C21: Fabricação de produtos farmoquímicos e farmacêuticos
- C23: Fabricação de produtos de minerais não metálicos (cement, ceramics, glass)
- C24: Metalurgia (steel, aluminum, non-ferrous)
- C17: Fabricação de celulose, papel e produtos de papel
- C19: Fabricação de coque, derivados de petróleo e biocombustíveis
- C20.1: Fabricação de produtos químicos inorgânicos (fertilizer intermediates)
